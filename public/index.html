      Your spool weights

      Subtracted from what the scale says to work out how much filament is left. Weigh a bare spool once and every roll of that brand will use it, in place of the published figures below.

      More options

      Only needed when a brand uses more than one spool. Leave these alone and the weight applies to everything of that brand.

      Published figures

      Collected from people who weighed their own spools. Useful as a starting point, but they go stale when a brand quietly changes its spool — anything above overrides these.
